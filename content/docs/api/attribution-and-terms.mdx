Using the BV API means accepting these terms. They apply to free and partner keys alike, and to figures from lookups, batches and bulk jobs.

## 1. Attribution

Wherever you show a BV from this API, show the words **BV by mxverify** beside it, as a working link to that site's `report_url`. Every `valued` and `unrated` result carries both in `attribution`.

```html
<span>BV $183 per link · <a href="https://mxverify.uk/publications/google.com">BV by mxverify</a></span>
```

- Any working link is fine. You may mark it `rel="nofollow"`.
- One attribution per figure, next to the figure. In a table of many sites, each row's figure has its own "BV by mxverify" link to that site's report.
- Do not remove, hide or shorten the words.

## 2. What BV is

BV is the ranking benefit of one new followed link, from a published model ([methodology](https://mxverify.uk/methodology)). It is **not a price**, not a promise of results, and not a statement about any site's quality. Do not present it as any of these: no "price", "cost" or "appraised value" labels, and no implying a site charges or should charge it.

## 3. Caching [#caching]

You may keep answers for up to **30 days**, then look them up again. BV moves as a site's traffic and authority are re-read and as the model changes; a figure older than 30 days may be misleading. Show the figure as it was answered; do not modify it.

## 4. No resale or bulk redistribution

Show BV in your own product, to your own users. Do not sell, license or redistribute BV figures in bulk, for example as a downloadable dataset or a data feed.

## 5. Fair use

Stay within your key's limits, and do not spread one use across several keys or accounts to get past them. A key used against these terms may be revoked.

## 6. As it is

Figures move when a site's record or the model changes, and the service may change or stop. The free tier is provided without warranty. Partner usage is charged as described in [Partner tier](/api/partner-tier); questions about a charge go to [support@bklink.uk](mailto:support@bklink.uk).
