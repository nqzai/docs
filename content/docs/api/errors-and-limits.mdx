## Limits

Limits are per key, per UTC day.

| | Free key | Partner key |
|---|---|---|
| Lookups (each target is one) | 1,000 | 50,000, or your account's own limit |
| New sites valued | 25 | 5,000, or your account's own limit |
| Targets per batch request | 100 | 100 |
| Targets per bulk job | — | 1,000 |
| Bulk jobs running at once | — | 3 |
| Live keys per account | 5 | 5 |

New valuations on every key also share a daily capacity across the service. Counting is approximate by a few calls in a busy minute.

A partner key's lookups and valuations beyond the free allowance run only while its account's wallet covers them; otherwise the key answers at the free limits. See [Partner tier](/api/partner-tier).

## Headers

Every successful lookup carries:

- `X-RateLimit-Limit`: the key's lookups a day.
- `X-RateLimit-Remaining`: lookups left today.

A `429` and a `valuing` answer carry `Retry-After` (seconds). For a daily limit, it is the time to the next UTC midnight.

## Errors

Errors answer a JSON body `{"error": "<code>", "message": "<plain words>", "docs": "https://mxverify.uk/api"}`.

| HTTP | `error` | When |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `bad_request` | The body is not `{"targets": [...]}` with at least one target. |
| 400 | `too_many_targets` | More than 100 targets in a batch, or 1,000 in a job. |
| 401 | `missing_key` | No key was sent. |
| 401 | `invalid_key` | The key does not exist or was revoked. |
| 403 | `partner_only` | A bulk job was asked for with a free key. |
| 404 | `not_found` | No such job for this key (jobs are kept a week). |
| 405 | `method_not_allowed` | The wrong method for the path. The `Allow` header names the right one. |
| 429 | `daily_limit` | The key's lookups for the day are used. |
| 429 | `funds_needed` | A partner key's free lookups are used and its wallet does not cover more. |
| 429 | `too_many_jobs` | Three of the key's jobs are already running. |
| 503 | `unavailable` | A job could not be started just now. Try again in a minute. |

## Handling them

- On `429`, wait for `Retry-After` before trying again.
- On `202` or `valuing`, ask again after `retry_after`; do not loop faster.
- Treat `unrated` and `not_found` as "no figure", and cache them like any other answer.
