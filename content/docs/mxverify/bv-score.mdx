---
title: The BV score
description: How the dollar value of one new followed link from a site is worked out, and what it is not.
---

**BV** (backlink value) is what a link is worth to rankings, in US dollars. A site's **BV score** is the value of one new followed link from it: what its links are typically worth before anything about your own site is known.

<Callout type="info" title="The authority">
  The live model, with its current version, every rate and threshold, is on [mxverify.uk/backlink-value](https://mxverify.uk/backlink-value) and [mxverify.uk/methodology](https://mxverify.uk/methodology). This page explains how to read it.
</Callout>

## Three factors

<Steps>
<Step>

### Site Strength (0 to 100)

Half the site's authority rating (Ahrefs Domain Rating), half its estimated monthly organic traffic on a log scale. It sets the ceiling: the most a followed editorial link from the site can be worth, halving for every fixed number of points below 100. mxverify shows strength in steps of 5. A site with no authority rating on record is not valued.

</Step>
<Step>

### Link level (A to D)

How much of the ceiling the kind of link the site gives keeps:

| Level | Kind of link | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| A | Editorial citation: linked because it is useful to readers | ×1 |
| B | Guest, sponsored or placed post; product or tool directory listing | ×0.5 |
| C | Press release, syndicated copy, profile, forum post, footer or sidebar link, and any nofollow link | ×0.1 |
| D | A site on one owner's link network | ×0.05 |

Where bklink has audited links on the site, those links are read to decide the level. Otherwise mxverify reads the site's homepage and up to ten articles and asks a calibrated model what kind of site it is and what kind of link it gives.

</Step>
<Step>

### Relevance

How close the linking site is to the site the link points to, from ×0.5 (unrelated) to ×1.25 (same topic). A report cannot know your site, so the score is worked out at ×1 and both ends of the range are printed beside it. In a bklink audit relevance is read link by link.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Caps

Two caps can lower the level a site is priced at, and a report names them after the level, for example "level B · nofollow":

- **nofollow**: a share of the site's links carry nofollow, sponsored or ugc, which BV counts as passing no ranking credit.
- **high-risk niche**: the site is likely adult, gambling, pharmacy or similar. Its links are valued for a buyer outside the niche; the report also gives the figure for a buyer inside it.

A site Google lists no page of is also capped at level C unless something on record shows Google indexing it.

## Unrated sites

A site with no authority rating on record is **unrated**: a link from it is not valued. A report shows no figure rather than $0, and the BV API answers `unrated`.

## What BV is not

- **Not a Domain Rating.** Domain Rating is Ahrefs' 0 to 100 score of a site's backlinks, and one input here. BV is a dollar value.
- **Not a price.** It says what a link is worth, not what a publication charges.
- **Not a guarantee.** A footer link, a nofollow link or a syndicated copy on the same site is worth a fraction of the figure.
- **Not a verdict on a site.** It is a model of ranking value, worked out the same way for every site.

## Versions

BV has a version (for example 2.8). Figures move when a site's record is re-read (each figure at most every 30 days) or when the model changes. Every API answer carries the version it was worked out under.
