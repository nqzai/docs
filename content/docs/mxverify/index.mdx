[mxverify.uk](https://mxverify.uk) answers one question about any website: **what is one new followed link from it worth?** The answer is its **BV score**, in US dollars, in a public site report that names the source and date of every figure.

mxverify is published by bklink and uses the same BV model bklink uses to value every link it audits.

## What you can do

- **Check a site.** Enter a website on [mxverify.uk](https://mxverify.uk). A site mxverify has not valued before is valued on the spot.
- **Read its report.** Every valued site has a report at `https://mxverify.uk/publications/<domain>`: the BV score, the link level, how its audited links behaved, how it links out, traffic, server and network, page speed, and similar sites. See [Site reports](/mxverify/site-reports).
- **Show the BV on your own site** with a [badge](/mxverify/badges), or programmatically with the [BV API](/api).
- **Browse the directories**: publications A to Z, sites by value of a link, shared servers and networks, top lists, and keyword pages.

## Principles

- **BV is what a link is worth, not a price.** Publications charge what they like; BV exists to show whether a price is worth paying.
- **No site pays to be listed, valued or left out.**
- **Every figure has a source.** Each report says where each figure came from and when it was read. Figures are re-read at most every 30 days.
