## Top-ups

- **Minimum top-up:** $10, by card through Stripe.
- **Card fees at cost:** card processing is deducted at cost (about 2.9% plus $0.30 for a US card), so a $10 top-up adds a little under $10. The exact amount is shown before you pay and on the receipt.
- **New accounts** start with free credit and need no card.

## Holds and charges

An action that runs over time, such as an audit, reserves its price when it starts and settles when it finishes. A run abandoned part-way has its reservation released automatically. Your wallet page lists every charge with what it was for.

## Refunds

Unspent balance is refundable at any time under the [refund policy](https://bklink.uk/legal/refunds). Instant indexing is not refunded once a URL is sent.
