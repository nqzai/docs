The link indexer sends pages to Google's indexing pipeline within minutes: your own pages, or the pages your backlinks sit on.

## Pages are read before you pay

Before it charges, bklink reads every page. A page that is dead, redirects, says noindex, names another canonical, is blocked by robots.txt, has under 300 words, or sits on a site Google lists nothing of is refused and named, with the reason. An indexer cannot override a page that tells Google not to index it.

## What it does not promise

Google decides whether and when to index a page. Indexing is **not guaranteed**, and a URL is **not refunded once sent**. If the indexer does not accept a batch, nothing is charged. Each sent URL comes with a link to check it in Google yourself.

## Price

$0.15 a URL. The usual path is to run the [index checker](/bklink/index-checker) first, then send the groups it marks likely or possible.
