title: How bklink values links
description: BV in an audit — Site Strength, link levels, relevance, and the discounts for repeated links and syndicated copies.
---

bklink values every link with **BV**, the same model mxverify publishes. In an audit each link is valued in full context: the target it points to, the other links from the same site, and copies of the same article.

<Callout type="info" title="The authority">
  The full model, with every rate, multiplier and worked example, is published at [bklink.uk/methodology](https://bklink.uk/methodology#bv) and read from the same code that prices reports. This page summarises it.
</Callout>

## The three factors

1. **Site Strength** (0 to 100): half the linking site's Ahrefs Domain Rating, half its estimated organic traffic on a log scale. It sets the most a link from the site can be worth. Traffic that was never measured counts as none until it is fetched. A site Ahrefs does not rate is valued at $0.
2. **Link level** (A to D): how likely search engines are to count the link.
   - **A** editorial citation: kept in full.
   - **B** guest, sponsored or placed post, or a directory listing.
   - **C** press release, syndicated copy, profile, forum post, footer or sidebar link, and **any nofollow link**.
   - **D** a site on one owner's link network.
3. **Relevance** of the linking page to the target, from unrelated to same topic.

## Discounts within one audit

- **Repeated links from one site.** A second link from the same site (a domain with its subdomains) keeps 20%, a third 10%, and each one after that half the one before, so one site is worth at most 1.4 times its best link.
- **Syndicated copies.** Every copy of a republished article other than the declared original keeps 5%.
- **Worth $0:** a missing link, a dead or non-indexable page, a site Ahrefs does not rate, or a link from the target's own site.

## Caps

- **Nofollow** caps a link at level C.
- **High-risk niche**: a site likely to be adult, gambling, pharmacy, CBD, payday loans, counterfeit goods or piracy is valued for a buyer outside the niche, at most level C.
- **Not in Google**: a site Google lists no page of (two `site:` searches at least 7 days apart found none, and nothing on record shows Google indexing it) is at most level C.

## What BV is not

BV is the ranking benefit a link carries, in dollars. It is not a price anyone charges, not what a link will do for one site, and not a statement about a site's quality.
