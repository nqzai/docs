## PDF and share link (free)

Any report can be exported as a PDF or sent as a share link. The report you send is the same evidence you audited. A share link's address is its credential: anyone with it can open the report, so send it only to the people it is for.

## White-label PDF

A white-label PDF has no bklink name, logo or links, and carries your agency's name if you add one.

- **Price:** $1.00 once per report. Every later white-label export of that report is free, with any filters.
- **Filters:** the PDF follows the filters you set on the report. The summary still describes the whole audit, and says so.
- **Credits that stay:** credits that data licences require, such as Ahrefs' for Domain Rating, remain on the page.
